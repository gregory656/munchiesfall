MUNCHIES FALL — THE NEXT-GEN GAME PROJECT

We are building a game that people can become addicted to playing, enjoy showing their friends, spend money on, and keep coming back to.

I want Munchies Fall to have:
- Insanely satisfying gameplay.
- Beautiful, polished, high-end 2D graphics.
- Funny characters with personality.
- Unexpected events and crazy gameplay combinations.
- Progression that makes players want just one more round.
- A proper economy, upgrades, unlockables and challenges.
- Multiplayer and competitive events eventually.
- Monetization that doesn't make the game annoying.
- An identity that can grow into a recognizable gaming brand.
And importantly, we will build it in small, manageable phases. No attempting to build the entire universe in Week 1.

1. Let's reinvent the game
I want to retain the Kenyan identity of Munchies Fall, but make the experience much bigger.



MUNCHIES FALL: CHAOS IN THE SOKO
A chaotic arcade adventure with a living market economy.

The new premise:

Something has gone terribly wrong in the Soko.

A mysterious machine has exploded above the market, sending food, money, magical ingredients, strange creatures and dangerous objects raining from the sky.

You are the new market champion.

Your mission is to collect valuable falling items, survive increasingly ridiculous challenges, build your own market empire and become the ultimate Soko legend.

But here's the twist:

The market has a personality. It changes, reacts to your decisions, creates opportunities and occasionally tries to destroy you.

This gives us a foundation for multiple game modes rather than one repetitive catching mechanic.
The five major gameplay worlds



1. The Falling Chaos

The original arcade experience. Catch food, dodge obstacles, chain combos, survive crazy weather and defeat bosses.
Core gameplay













2. Soko Tycoon

Own stalls, hire workers, manufacture food, negotiate prices, expand your market and build an empire.
Long-term progression













3. Food Boss Battles

Fight giant samosa monsters, angry cooking pots, flying chapati dragons and other ridiculous bosses.
Unique identity













4. Soko Wars

Compete against other players, complete challenges, steal power-ups and climb competitive rankings.
Social competition













5. The Munchie Universe

Collect characters, discover rare food creatures, unlock skins, complete collections and build your personal identity.
Collection system



These are the eventual worlds. We will NOT build all five immediately.

Phase 6 — Multiplayer and online systems
We should not attempt real-time multiplayer until the core game works.

Build online features in this order:
1. Player accounts.
2. Cloud save.
3. Daily challenges.
4. Global leaderboards.
5. Friends and profiles.
6. Ghost races against recorded player runs.
7. Asynchronous competitions.
8. Real-time multiplayer.
9. Weekly tournaments.
10. Server-side score verification.
A key idea: Soko World Championship.

Every weekend, players compete in a global event. The top performers receive exclusive cosmetics, titles and in-game rewards.

I would avoid promising real M-Pesa cash prizes in the initial release. Cash competitions introduce fraud, regulatory, payment and operational risks. We can investigate those separately if the game earns enough to justify them.

9. How we will work with Antigravity
I want us to use Antigravity as our development assistant, but with strict engineering discipline.

We will use this workflow:
1. I prepare a technical task.
2. You give it to Antigravity.
3. Antigravity implements that task.
4. We inspect the generated code.
5. We run the game in Visual Studio/Unity.
6. We test the feature.
7. We fix bugs.
8. We commit the working version to Git.
9. We proceed to the next task.
One feature at a time. No blindly generating 50 files and hoping they work.

Every task should have:
- Clear requirements.
- Files to create or modify.
- Expected behaviour.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Testing instructions.
- No breaking existing functionality.
